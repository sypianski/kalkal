Hijri calendar variant

Tabular variants differ in which years of the 30-year cycle are leap. Wüstenfeld (IIc) is the standard for historical scholarship.

Byzantine calendar variant

Variants differ in the epoch of Creation (Anno Mundi) and the start of the new year.

Seleucid calendar variant

Macedonian and Babylonian variants use the same era but differ in the month order.

Yazdgerdi (Zoroastrian) calendar variant

Three calendar systems sharing the Zoroastrian wandering year but starting from different reform epochs.
