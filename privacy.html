Privacy Policy

This policy covers the kalkal apps for iOS and Android and the website kalkal.online.

The apps collect no data. They have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. All date conversions are computed on your device and the apps work fully offline.

What is stored on your device. Your settings (language, default calendars, calendar variants, copy format) and your recent conversion history are kept only in local storage on your device. They are never transmitted anywhere and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear the site data in your browser.

Links. Links to external websites, including eclipse maps on kalkal.online, open in your browser and are subject to the policies of those sites.

Website. kalkal.online is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes standard technical request data (such as IP address and browser type) in order to serve and protect the site. The site sets no tracking cookies.
