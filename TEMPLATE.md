Buttons
- FROM: calendar to convert from, by default: hijri
- YEAR: year to convert from, typed manually from the in-app keyboard
- TO: calendar to convert to, by default AD (Julian+Gregorian)
- RESULT: date converted
- MONTH: month to convert from, dependent on FROM, drop-down list
- DAY: day to convert from, typed manually from the in-app keyboard
- CONVERT: button that makes the conversion happen
