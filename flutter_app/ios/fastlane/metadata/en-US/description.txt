Kalkal converts dates between more than twenty historical and living calendar systems, built for historians, students and anyone reading sources dated in another era.

Enter a date in one calendar and see it in all the others at once: Hijri, Gregorian, Julian, Coptic, Ethiopian, Solar Hijri, Byzantine, Syriac (Seleucid), Egyptian, Armenian, Yazdgerdi, Babylonian and more.

FEATURES
• Four tabular Hijri variants (IIc Wüstenfeld, IIa, IIIa, IVa), with the differences between them shown side by side
• Variants for the Byzantine, Seleucid and Yazdgerdi eras
• Convert a single day, a whole month or a whole year to a date range
• Day of the week, moon phase and historical astronomical events (comets, supernovae, conjunctions, solar eclipses) for the converted date
• Arabic month names in ISO 233 transliteration
• Copy results in several formats; recent conversions kept in history
• Interface in English, Polish and Esperanto
• Works fully offline — no account, no ads, no tracking

Every conversion runs through the Julian Day Number, and the methodology and sources are documented inside the app.
